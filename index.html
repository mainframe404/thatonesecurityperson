About

I fell into security operations and never really left the floor. Since 2018 I've worked across incident response, identity and access management, and vulnerability management — the unglamorous, constant work that keeps an enterprise from becoming a headline.

At Netskope, I helped take the Security Operations Center from a lean, three-person team stretched across every function to a group that automates the equivalent of an extra analyst's workload every week. Now my focus has shifted toward threat and vulnerability management — closing the gap between "we found it" and "we fixed it," at scale.

I'm currently finishing an MBA concentrated in AI strategy, which mostly means I spend evenings arguing with case studies about where AI actually earns its keep in a security program, and where it's just expensive theater.
